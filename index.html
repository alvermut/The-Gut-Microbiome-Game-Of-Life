Rules

  • Birth: 3, 4 living neighbors.
  • Survival: 4, 5, 6 living neighbors.
  • Death: all other counts.
  • All living cell types count equally; births inherit a neighboring type.
  • Living niches persist and turn adjacent surviving nutrients into matching free microbes.
  • Mucus tightens survival across the outer 10 squares; leaky gut switches it off.
  • Antibiotic deactivates 25% of niches and removes 80% of free microbes.
  • Probiotic restores 25% of vacant niche sites and adds 25–70 microbes of one type in connected blobs of 3–8, as space allows.
  • SEQUENCE counts free microbes across the whole field, leaves cells in place, and ends the run.
